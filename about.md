Sanaan Wani is a Security Engineer at Amazon and a computer science student at the University of South Florida, where he is Blue Team Captain of CyberHerd, the university's cybersecurity competition team.

His research targets the infrastructure that runs large language models: agent frameworks, inference servers, workflow orchestrators, and the serialization formats they trust. Most of it comes down to reachability. An auth gated code execution sink is a bug; the same sink reachable before authentication is a critical. Seven CVEs have been assigned from that work, against projects including Ray, Open WebUI, Kestra, Hatchet, Budibase, and trigger.dev. The full list, with root cause and fix for each, is on the [home page](/).

At DEF CON he took first place at AI Village and second at Adversary Village in 2026, and first at Adversary Village the year before.
